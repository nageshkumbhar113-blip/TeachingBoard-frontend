FAQ

Good to know

How long does video approval take?

Our team reviews new submissions within 24–48 hours. You'll see the status update on your dashboard.

Can I replace a video later?

Yes — editing sends it for re-approval, but the version students currently see stays live until the new one is approved.

What happens if my subscription expires?

Your videos are hidden from students (not deleted) until you renew — everything comes back instantly.

Will my YouTube channel be shown to students?

No — your channel link is used only for our internal verification, never shown publicly.
