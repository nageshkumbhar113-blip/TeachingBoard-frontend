Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for Nks EduOrbit subscriptions.

1. Free Trial

New students get a free trial period (as shown at registration) before any payment is required. Use this time to try the Service before subscribing.

2. Subscription Payments

Subscriptions (monthly/yearly) are digital services that activate immediately on successful payment, giving instant access to paid content.

3. Refund Eligibility

  • If a payment was debited but the subscription did not activate due to a technical error, you are eligible for a full refund. Contact us with your payment reference within 7 days and we will verify and refund the amount.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same subscription period by mistake, the duplicate charge will be refunded in full.
  • Once a subscription has activated and its content has been accessed, it is non-refundable for change of mind, since it is a digital service delivered instantly.

4. Cancellation

You may stop renewing a subscription at any time by not renewing after the current billing period ends — there is no lock-in. Cancelling does not refund the time already paid for in the current period.

5. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days.

6. How to Request a Refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your registered name, Code, and payment reference/transaction ID, and we'll review your request.
