1. Purpose

The Platform offers an educational app for Maharashtra State Board students (notes, exercises, MCQ tests and word tests). The Partner wishes to recommend the app to their audience and earn a commission on paid subscriptions made through their unique Teacher Code.

2. Teacher Code and Link

  • The Platform gives the Partner a unique Teacher Code and referral link once the Partner’s account is approved.

  • A student is linked to only one Partner code. The first valid code stays linked, unless the Platform’s records show a technical error.
  • The Platform’s records are final for deciding which student is linked to which Partner.

3. Commission

  • If the Partner disputes a payout, the Partner must inform the Platform in writing within 15 days of the payout date.
