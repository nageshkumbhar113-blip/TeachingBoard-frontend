1. Account

  • A Teacher account starts as pending and works only after the Platform approves it. The Platform may set a validity date, and may block an account for misuse.
  • The Teacher keeps the PIN private and is responsible for activity on the account.

2. Free plan and Paper Builder

  • On the free plan the Teacher can build 4 papers per batch.
  • Unlimited Paper Builder for a batch is available once at least 10 students of that batch, linked to the Teacher, have paid, or after the Teacher pays for students of that batch in the app (until their access ends). The Platform may change these numbers with notice.

3. Students linked to the Teacher

  • Student accounts created by the Teacher are made only after full payment. Only the Platform can change a student’s name or mobile number or remove an account.
  • The Teacher sees the results and activity of linked students to help teach them, and must keep that information private and use it only for teaching.

4. Discount when the Teacher pays for students

  • The Teacher may create student accounts in the app and pay for them online. The Platform may give the Teacher a discount on the course price for such payments (a percentage or a fixed amount per student), as set by the Platform for that Teacher and shown in the app before payment.
  • The discount applies only to payments the Teacher makes. When a student or parent pays the Platform directly, the Teacher receives no money, share or reward of any kind.
  • No payment is taken for a student who already has the same course active; that student is only linked to the Teacher.
  • The Platform may change or end a Teacher’s discount at any time; a change does not affect payments already made. Refunds, if any, follow the Platform’s refund policy.

5. Honest promotion and fraud

  • Make no false promises about marks or results. Do not charge students more than the Platform’s course price for the access the Teacher bought for them.
  • No fake accounts, accounts for people who are not real students, sharing or selling of codes, or encouraging refunds. The Platform may end the discount and suspend the account.

6. Privacy

Student data belongs to the Platform and is handled under the Privacy Policy. The Teacher will not copy, share or misuse student personal data.
