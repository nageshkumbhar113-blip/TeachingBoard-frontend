Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nks EduOrbit ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, student app, and admin app (together, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Registration details: student name, class/batch, parent contact number, and email address.
  • Payment information: subscription plan selected and payment status. Actual card/UPI/bank details are collected and processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay — we never store your card or bank details.
  • Usage data: quiz/test attempts, scores, notes viewed, and app activity, used to show progress and reports.
  • Device data: a device identifier used to secure your account (device-binding) so a login cannot be shared across multiple devices at once.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your student account and subscription.
  • To deliver learning content (notes, quizzes, tests, vocabulary) and track progress.
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay and send payment/receipt confirmations.
  • To respond to support requests sent via WhatsApp or email.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. Information is shared only with:

  • Razorpay (our payment gateway), solely to process payments.
  • Service providers who host our app and database (e.g., cloud hosting), strictly to operate the Service.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on secured cloud servers. The app also stores some data locally on your device (offline mode) so it works without continuous internet access. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, though no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Nks EduOrbit is an educational app used by school students, typically registered by a parent/guardian. Parents are responsible for the accuracy of registration details submitted on behalf of their child.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
